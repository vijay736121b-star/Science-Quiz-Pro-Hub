Science Quiz Pro – Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026

Science Quiz Pro is an educational Android application designed to help users learn and test their science knowledge. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Personal Information

Science Quiz Pro does not require an account or login and does not ask users to provide their name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, or payment information.

2. Quiz Results and History

Quiz scores, answers, performance summaries, and quiz history are stored locally on the user's Android device so the app can provide its history and review features. This information is not intentionally uploaded by the app as part of the quiz-history feature.

3. Permissions

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, or phone-state runtime permissions.

4. Internet and Third-Party Services

The current Android build includes Google/Firebase libraries. Network access may therefore be used by those libraries for their supported services and security functions when applicable. Science Quiz Pro does not use the quiz-history database to create a user account or sell personal information.

5. Advertising and Sale of Personal Information

Science Quiz Pro is not designed to sell users' personal information. The current app experience does not intentionally use advertising or behavioral profiling features.

6. Children's Privacy

Science Quiz Pro is an educational app. It does not knowingly request personal information from children. Parents or guardians who have privacy questions may contact the developer using the contact information provided in the app store listing.

7. Data Security

The app uses Android's application sandbox and local storage mechanisms for locally stored quiz information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's data practices or features change. The effective date shown above will be updated when material changes are made.
